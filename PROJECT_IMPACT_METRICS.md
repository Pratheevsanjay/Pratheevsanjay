### Project Highlights
- **Accuracy:** 92.5%
- **F1 Score:** 0.92
- **Technique:** TF-IDF + Logistic Regression
- **Dataset:** ~5,000+ news articles
- **Classes:** Binary (Fake/Real)

### Performance Breakdown
- **Precision:** 91.8% (Correctly identified fake news)
- **Recall:** 93.2% (Caught most fake articles)
